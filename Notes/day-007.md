# BeeLabs Day 7 — Data Analysis Foundations

**Date:** September 1, 2026

## What I Built

I created a sales dataset and analyzed it in Microsoft Excel.

### Project Files

- `Projects/003-Data-Analyst/sales_data.csv`
- `Projects/003-Data-Analyst/sales_analysis.xlsx`

## Skills I Practiced

- Creating a structured CSV dataset
- Opening CSV data in Excel
- Calculating revenue with `=C2*D2`
- Copying formulas with the fill handle
- Calculating total revenue with `=SUM(E2:E8)`
- Formatting data as an Excel table
- Sorting revenue from largest to smallest
- Creating and troubleshooting a column chart
- Interpreting business results

## Results

- Total revenue: **$4,298**
- Highest-revenue product: **Laptop — $2,550**
- Lowest-revenue product: **Pens — $40**
- Created a **Revenue by Product** chart

## Key Lesson

A data analyst does more than calculate numbers. An analyst organizes data, checks that calculations are correct, creates visuals, and explains what the results mean.
