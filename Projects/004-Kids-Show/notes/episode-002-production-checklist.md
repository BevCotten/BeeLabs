## Important: Follow This Order

1. **Lock Dr. Nia first.** Do not generate finished dental-office scenes until her face, hair, complexion, outfit, jewelry, age, and proportions are approved.
2. **Lock both backgrounds.** Use one established home bathroom and one established dental office throughout the episode.
3. **Write the complete script.** Confirm every speaker and line before cutting audio.
4. **Create one complete master audio track.** Listen to the entire track before clipping anything.
5. **Create a timing sheet.** Record the exact start and end time of every line.
6. **Create the still panels.** Check continuity before spending animation credits.
7. **Animate one speaker at a time.** Use silent B-roll to cover group dialogue and transitions.
8. **Assemble in Clipchamp.** Add sound effects, music, thought visuals, and transitions during editing.
9. **Watch the entire export.** Check audio, lip-sync, visual gaps, reflections, duplicate objects, and character consistency.

## Audio File Naming

Use the same long, searchable naming pattern for every audio clip:

- `adventures-with-limi-episode-002-scene-02-limi-the-dentist.mp3`
- `adventures-with-limi-episode-002-scene-06-dr-nia-introduction.mp3`
- `adventures-with-limi-episode-002-scene-12-limi-show-me-again-please.mp3`

Never overwrite a clip that needs correcting. Add a clear suffix such as `-extended`, `-corrected`, or `-v2` until the correct version is confirmed.

## Panel File Naming

Use the filenames already listed in the storyboard. Every visual begins with:

`adventures-with-limi-episode-002-scene-##-`

Do not use vague names such as `new-panel.png`, `final-final.png`, or `image-3.png`.

## Hedra Rules for Every Dialogue Clip

- Only the named speaker lip-syncs.
- Every other visible person keeps their mouth fully closed while listening.
- When possible, keep all other speaking characters outside the frame.
- Do not ask Hedra to assign several voices across a group image.
- Keep the camera steady unless a camera move is essential to the story.
- Do not add people, objects, limbs, teeth, shoes, tools, text, or decorations.
- Do not animate mirrors or create new reflections.
- Tiggy remains completely motionless and inanimate.

## Credit-Saving Plan

- Use still panels for establishing shots and reaction moments.
- Animate only the lines that need visible lip-sync.
- Use silent B-roll for the chair rising, the overhead light, tool demonstrations, and the smile reveal.
- Test one short Dr. Nia clip and one short Limi clip before generating longer scenes.
- Approve each character and background before creating multiple shots from it.
