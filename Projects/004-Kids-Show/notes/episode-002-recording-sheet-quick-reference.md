# Episode 002 Recording Sheet — Limi’s First Dental Visit

**Series:** *Adventures with Limi*  
**Episode:** 002  
**Status:** Script approved — recording phase  
**Target runtime:** 6–7 minutes  
**Core phrase:** “Show me first, then I can try.”

## Recording Instructions

- Record in a quiet room with the same microphone position and gain for each actor.
- Record each numbered line as a separate clean take when practical.
- Leave about 1–2 seconds of silence before and after each take.
- Record at least 2 takes of every dialogue line; use Take 3 when a line needs another performance option.
- Do not record stage directions, audience pauses, silent B-roll, or sound effects as dialogue.
- Keep delivery warm, natural, and preschool-friendly. Do not rush.
- Preserve intentional pauses shown with ellipses.
- Mark the preferred take in the **Best Take** column.
- Suggested file format: `E002_[Character]_S[Scene]_L[Line]_T[Take].wav`
