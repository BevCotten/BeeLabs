# Episode 001 — Jazz Voice Recording Sheet

## Series

*Adventures with Limi*

## Voice Direction

Jazz sounds like a real 16-year-old big sister: confident, outspoken, caring, protective, and effortlessly cool. She guides Limi without lecturing or talking down to him.

- Use Mimi’s natural teenage voice and personality.
- Keep explanations conversational and affectionate.
- Let playful lines feel spontaneous rather than overly performed.
- Speak slowly enough for children ages 2–4 while preserving natural rhythm.
- Record one scene at a time and leave about two seconds between lines.
- Keep AutoPitch off for spoken dialogue.

## Scene 3 — Jazz Notices

**Direction:** Playfully observant at first, then gentle and reassuring.

1. “Hmm. Your eyes are wide.”
2. “Your shoulders are way up here.”
3. “And you’re squeezing those shoes very tightly.”
4. “Sometimes our bodies tell us how we feel before our words do.”
5. “That sounds like nervous feelings.”
6. “You can be nervous and brave at the same time.”
7. “Exactly. Being brave means trying even when something feels new.”
8. “I have an idea. Let’s show Tiggy what happens during a haircut.”

## Scene 4 — Practicing With Tiggy

**Direction:** Energetic, rhythmic, and playful. Make the four steps easy for toddlers to remember.

1. “Haircuts happen one step at a time.”
2. “First, you sit in the chair.”
3. “Next, the barber puts on the cape.”
4. “Then, you hear the clippers.”
5. “Last, you look in the mirror.”
6. “Can you remember the steps with us?”
7. “See? Now we know what to expect.”
8. “Absolutely.”

## Scene 5 — Team Limi at Breakfast

**Direction:** Supportive and confident.

1. “And you already know how to ask for a break.”

## Scene 6 — Arriving at the Barbershop

**Direction:** Curious and encouraging. Help Limi look around without rushing him.

1. “What do you notice?”
2. “That’s the last step!”

## Scene 7 — Meeting Ms. Toni

**Direction:** Proud of Limi’s choice, but keep it natural and brief.

1. “You got to choose how you wanted to say hello.”

## Scene 8 — Hearing the Clippers

**Direction:** Calm, slow, and easy for the audience to follow during the breathing exercise.

1. “Let’s take our slow breath.”
2. “Smell the flower.”
3. “Now blow out the candle.”
4. “Can everyone help Limi?”

## Scene 9 — “Break, Please”

**Direction:** Caring and curious. Let Limi identify what he needs.

1. “What would help your body feel better?”

## Scene 10 — Wiggle Break

**Direction:** Fun, energetic, and inviting.

1. “Everybody stand up if you can!”
2. “Now stomp three times!”

## Scene 11 — Still-as-a-Statue Game

**Direction:** Playful but controlled; then lead the count slowly.

1. “Let’s play the Still-as-a-Statue Game!”
2. “Can everyone help Limi count slowly to five?”

## Scene 12 — Mirror Reveal

**Direction:** Big-sister pride and excitement.

1. “That’s you, little bro!”

## Scene 13 — Light It Up

**Direction:** Begin playfully, then deliver the lesson phrase clearly and confidently.

1. “And what did you do when your neck felt itchy?”
2. “I can ask questions.”
