# Episode 002 Script — Limi’s First Dental Visit

## Episode Information

**Series:** *Adventures with Limi*  
**Episode:** 002  
**Title:** “Limi’s First Dental Visit”  
**Audience:** Ages 2–4  
**Estimated Runtime:** 6–7 minutes  
**Core Lesson:** When something is new, I can ask to see it first.  
**Repeatable Phrase:** “Show me first, then I can try.”  
**Theme Song:** “Adventures with Limi”  
**Theme Song Creators and Vocalists:** Bee and James Cotten  
**Music Artist/Producer:** James “J-Killz” Cotten

## Learning Goals

* Learn what happens during a routine dental visit
* Practice asking questions about something unfamiliar
* Learn the simple sequence: ask, look, try
* Understand that children can ask to see a tool before it is used
* Practice making small choices during a new experience
* Build confidence through preparation and participation
* Reinforce daily tooth brushing with help from a grown-up

## Featured Characters

* **Limi**
* **Maya**
* **Limi Sr.**
* **Jazz**
* **Dr. Miles**
* **Tiggy**, Limi’s beloved stuffed tiger and comfort companion

---

## Opening Title Sequence

**Music Cue:** “Adventures with Limi” theme song (approximately 21 seconds)

A warm purple-and-electric-lime glow sweeps across the screen. Limi follows a trail of tiny lights through quick glimpses of everyday adventures with his family. He turns toward the audience, smiles, and raises one hand as the series title appears.

**ON-SCREEN TITLE:** *Adventures with Limi*

The final musical beat carries us into the family bathroom.

---

## Scene 1 — Morning Brush

**Location:** Family home bathroom  
**Storyboard Panel:** 1

Morning sunlight fills the bathroom.

Limi stands safely on his step stool at the sink while Maya helps him brush his teeth. Limi watches himself in the mirror.

**MAYA**
Brush, brush, brush.

Maya gently guides the toothbrush around Limi’s teeth.

**MAYA**
Front teeth...back teeth...and way in the back.

Limi opens his mouth wide.

**LIMI**
Aaaaah!

He makes an exaggerated lion face in the mirror.

**LIMI**
ROAR!

Maya laughs.

**MAYA**
That is one serious brushing lion.

Limi grins around the toothbrush.

**MAYA**
Little kids need a grown-up to help make sure every tooth gets clean.

Limi opens wide again.

**LIMI**
Roar!

---

## Scene 2 — Dentist Day

**Location:** Family home bathroom  
**Storyboard Panel:** 2

Limi finishes brushing.

Maya speaks from off-camera while Limi remains in close-up.

**MAYA (O.S.)**
And those teeth are going to be extra clean for Dr. Miles today.

Limi pauses.

He slowly lowers his toothbrush.

His eyes widen and his shoulders lift.

**LIMI**
The dentist?

Limi looks toward the audience.

**LIMI**
Have you been to the dentist before?

**PAUSE FOR AUDIENCE RESPONSE**

Limi looks thoughtful.

**LIMI**
I have questions.

---

## Scene 3 — Limi Has Questions

**Location:** Family home  
**Storyboard Panel:** 3

Limi sits with his family. Tiggy rests motionless beside him.

**LIMI**
What happens at the dentist?

Maya answers in a separate close-up.

**MAYA**
The dentist checks your teeth and helps keep your mouth healthy.

Limi Sr. answers in his own shot.

**LIMI SR.**
You’ll see a special chair and a bright light so the dentist can see your teeth.

Jazz smiles.

**JAZZ**
And if you see something you don’t know, you can ask about it.

Limi looks at Tiggy, then back toward his family.

**LIMI**
What if I don’t know what something does?

**MAYA**
Then you ask.

**LIMI SR.**
You don’t have to pretend you understand.

Jazz raises three fingers.

**JAZZ**
Actually, I have a plan for new things.

---

## Scene 4 — The New-Thing Plan

**Location:** Family home  
**Storyboard Panel:** 4

Jazz holds up one finger.

**JAZZ**
First—ask.

She holds up a second finger.

**JAZZ**
Next—look.

She holds up a third.

**JAZZ**
Then—try when you’re ready.

Limi copies her three fingers.

**LIMI**
Ask...look...try.

**JAZZ**
Exactly.

Jazz speaks slowly and clearly.

**JAZZ**
Show me first...

Cut to Limi.

**LIMI**
...then I can try!

Jazz smiles.

**JAZZ**
That’s your new-thing plan.

Limi turns toward the audience and raises three fingers.

**LIMI**
Can you do it with me?

**LIMI**
Ask!

**PAUSE**

**LIMI**
Look!

**PAUSE**

**LIMI**
Try!

**PAUSE**

**LIMI**
Show me first, then I can try!

Limi picks up Tiggy.

**LIMI**
Come on, Tiggy. We have a plan.

---

## Scene 5 — Arriving at the Dentist

**Location:** Pediatric dental office  
**Storyboard Panel:** 5

**SILENT B-ROLL**

The family enters the pediatric dental office.

Limi holds Maya’s hand and carries Tiggy securely under his other arm. Jazz and Limi Sr. follow.

Limi looks around carefully.

He notices the reception desk, chairs, books, dental posters, and people working calmly.

Limi squeezes Maya’s hand once.

Maya smiles down at him.

Limi looks at Tiggy and takes another step forward.

**MUSIC:** Light, curious instrumental transition.

---

## Scene 6 — Meeting Dr. Miles

**Location:** Pediatric dental office  
**Storyboard Panel:** 6

Dr. Miles sits or kneels at Limi’s height several feet away. His hands remain relaxed and visible.

**DR. MILES**
Hi, Limi. I’m Dr. Miles.

Dr. Miles smiles warmly.

**DR. MILES**
I check little teeth and care for big smiles.

Limi listens while holding Tiggy.

**DR. MILES**
Before I do anything, I can tell you what it is and show you how it works.

Limi’s shoulders relax slightly.

---

## Scene 7 — Fist Bump

**Location:** Pediatric dental office  
**Storyboard Panel:** 7

Limi smiles at Dr. Miles.

**LIMI**
It’s nice to meet you, Dr. Miles. Can we do a fist bump?

Dr. Miles smiles.

**DR. MILES**
Thank you for telling me which greeting you’re comfortable with.

Dr. Miles holds out his fist.

Limi gives Dr. Miles a fist bump.

---

## Scene 8 — Choosing the Chair

**Location:** Dental treatment room  
**Storyboard Panel:** 8

The orange dental chair waits in the center of the room.

Dr. Miles gestures toward it.

**DR. MILES**
This is the dental chair. You can climb in with Mom right beside you, or you can sit on Mom’s lap.

Cut to Limi.

He looks at the chair.

He looks at Maya.

Maya stays close without rushing him.

**MAYA**
You choose, baby. I’m right here with you.

Limi hugs Tiggy, thinks, then points toward the chair.

**LIMI**
The chair.

Maya smiles warmly and gives his hand a gentle squeeze.

**MAYA**
Okay, my love. I’ll be right beside you.

Limi climbs into the chair. Tiggy rests motionless on his lap.

**SILENT B-ROLL:** The dental chair slowly rises.

Limi’s eyes grow wide.

Then he smiles.

**LIMI**
I am on a rocket ship! Blast off!

---

## Scene 9 — The Bright Light

**Location:** Dental treatment room  
**Storyboard Panel:** 9

Limi notices the overhead dental light.

His eyes follow it.

Dr. Miles notices.

**DR. MILES**
This light helps me see your smile.

Dr. Miles positions the light toward his own gloved hand first.

A bright circle appears on the glove.

**DR. MILES**
Would you like to see it first?

Limi nods.

**LIMI**
Show me first.

Dr. Miles lets Limi look at the circle of light.

**DR. MILES**
It’s bright, but it doesn’t touch your teeth.

Limi studies it.

**DR. MILES**
Ready to try?

**LIMI**
Then I can try.

**SILENT B-ROLL:** Dr. Miles carefully positions the light above Limi.

Limi looks up curiously.

---

## Scene 10 — The Tiny Mirror

**Location:** Dental treatment room  
**Storyboard Panel:** 10

Dr. Miles holds up a small round dental mirror.

**DR. MILES**
This is a tiny mirror for looking at teeth.

He turns it slowly so Limi can see its shape.

**DR. MILES**
See? It’s just a little mirror.

Dr. Miles gently touches the back of the mirror to his own gloved fingertip.

Limi leans forward to study it.

**LIMI**
Tiny mirror.

**DR. MILES**
Would you like me to use it to look at your teeth?

Limi thinks.

**LIMI**
I looked...and now I can try.

Dr. Miles smiles.

**DR. MILES**
You got it.

---

## Scene 11 — Count Limi’s Teeth

**Location:** Dental treatment room  
**Storyboard Panel:** 11

Limi reclines comfortably in the chair. Maya’s hand rests reassuringly beside him.

Dr. Miles speaks from off-camera.

**DR. MILES (O.S.)**
Can you open wide like a lion?

Limi opens his mouth.

**DR. MILES (O.S.)**
Whoa! That is a big lion mouth.

Limi’s eyes smile.

**DR. MILES (O.S.)**
Can everybody help me count while I look?

**DR. MILES (O.S.)**
One...two...three...four...

**PAUSE FOR AUDIENCE COUNTING**

The count continues playfully as Dr. Miles checks different areas.

**DR. MILES (O.S.)**
Keep counting!

**PAUSE FOR AUDIENCE RESPONSE**

Dr. Miles finishes the check.

**DR. MILES (O.S.)**
Great job, Limi. You kept your mouth open while I counted.

Limi closes his mouth and beams.

---

## Scene 12 — Show Me Again, Please

**Location:** Dental treatment room  
**Storyboard Panel:** 12

A gentle dental-tool sound begins.

**SOUND:** Soft suction sound.

Limi’s eyes widen.

He raises one hand.

Dr. Miles stops immediately.

**SOUND STOPS**

Cut to Limi-only close-up.

**LIMI**
Show me again, please.

**SILENT B-ROLL:** Dr. Miles holds the suction tool away from Limi’s face and demonstrates it safely.

Dr. Miles speaks in a separate shot.

**DR. MILES**
This little straw takes away extra water.

He demonstrates the sound again away from Limi.

**SOUND:** Soft suction sound.

**DR. MILES**
Would you like to try now?

Cut to Limi.

He looks at the tool.

He remembers his three steps and raises three fingers.

**LIMI**
I asked.

One finger.

**LIMI**
I looked.

Two fingers.

Limi smiles.

**LIMI**
Now I can try.

**SILENT B-ROLL:** Dr. Miles resumes gently while Limi remains calm.

---

## Scene 13 — Sparkly Smile, Light It Up

**Location:** Dental treatment room  
**Storyboard Panel:** 13

The visit is finished.

Limi sits upright and looks at his clean smile in a handheld mirror.

His eyes grow wide.

**LIMI**
Ooooh!

He grins at his reflection.

**LIMI**
My teeth are shiny!

Dr. Miles smiles.

**DR. MILES**
And you did a great job asking questions.

Jazz raises three fingers.

**JAZZ**
What was your new-thing plan?

Limi raises his own three fingers.

**LIMI**
I asked...

He raises the first finger.

**LIMI**
I looked...

Second finger.

**LIMI**
And then I tried!

Third finger.

Limi turns toward the audience.

**LIMI**
Did you practice with me?

**PAUSE FOR AUDIENCE RESPONSE**

**LIMI**
We did it!

Maya smiles proudly.

**MAYA**
You asked when you wanted to understand something.

**LIMI SR.**
And you tried when you felt ready.

**DR. MILES**
That is a great way to learn about something new.

Limi hugs Tiggy.

A gentle electric-lime glow shines from the symbol on Limi’s shirt.

Tiny sparkling lights appear around him.

**LIMI**
When something is new—

Cut to Jazz.

**JAZZ**
I can ask.

Cut to Maya.

**MAYA**
I can look.

Cut to Limi Sr.

**LIMI SR.**
And I can try when I’m ready.

Cut back to Limi.

**LIMI**
Show me first, then I can try!

The family celebrates.

**LIMI**
We learned something new—

**EVERYONE**
—and now we shine!

**MUSIC CUE:** A short instrumental reprise of the “Adventures with Limi” theme plays over the closing title card.

**ON-SCREEN TITLE:** *Adventures with Limi*
