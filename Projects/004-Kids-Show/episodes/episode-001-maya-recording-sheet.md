# Episode 001 — Maya Voice Recording Sheet

## Series

*Adventures with Limi*

## Performance Guide

- Use Bee’s natural adult voice rather than Limi’s raised toddler voice.
- Maya sounds warm, observant, reassuring, and emotionally present.
- Keep her support calm and genuine rather than overly sweet or theatrical.
- Record one scene at a time and leave about two seconds between lines.
- Keep AutoPitch off for spoken dialogue.

## Scene 2 — The Haircut Announcement

**Direction:** Cheerful morning warmth, followed by calm reassurance when Maya notices Limi’s reaction.

1. “Good morning, my little light!”
2. “Pick your favorite sneakers. After breakfast, we’re going to see Ms. Toni for your haircut.”
3. “That’s right. Ms. Toni is going to make your curls look fresh.”
4. “Okay. We’ll all be there with you.”

## Scene 5 — Team Limi at Breakfast

**Direction:** Practical and reassuring. Maya reminds Limi that he has control and can communicate.

1. “You can also tell Ms. Toni if you need her to stop.”

## Scene 6 — Arriving at the Barbershop

**Direction:** Quiet, patient, and encouraging. Do not rush Limi.

1. “We’re here.”
2. “Just like the one we practiced with.”

## Scene 9 — “Break, Please”

**Direction:** Proud and affirming without interrupting the calm moment.

1. “You used your words.”

## Scene 10 — Wiggle Break

**Direction:** Lead a slow, calming transition after the playful movement.

1. “And take one slow breath.”

## Scene 12 — Mirror Reveal

**Direction:** Warm pride. Maya is delighted by Limi’s confidence, not only his haircut.

1. “Look at that smile.”

## Scene 13 — Light It Up

**Direction:** Celebratory for the first line, then clear and rhythmic for the lesson recap.

1. “You listened to your body.”
2. “I can listen to my body.”
