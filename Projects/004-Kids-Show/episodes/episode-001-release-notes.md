## Story summary

Limi prepares for his first haircut and notices that his body feels nervous. His family helps him identify those feelings without dismissing them. After practicing the haircut steps with Tiggy, Limi visits Ms. Toni's barbershop, chooses how to greet her, asks questions, listens to the clippers from a comfortable distance, uses the words “Break, please,” and returns when his body feels ready.

## Learning objectives

Children are encouraged to:

- Recognize physical signs of nervousness.
- Understand that nervousness and bravery can happen together.
- Ask questions about unfamiliar experiences.
- Use clear words to communicate bodily needs.
- Practice slow breathing and movement breaks.
- Approach new experiences one step at a time.

## Production retrospective

### What worked

- The story has a complete emotional arc from uncertainty to confidence.
- Each family member supports Limi in a distinct way.
- Ms. Toni models consent, patience, and respect for body cues.
- Repeated phrases reinforce the episode's preschool learning goals.
- Purple and electric lime create a recognizable series identity.

### Improvements for Episode 002

- Design scenes around one visible speaker whenever possible.
- Reduce multi-character lip-sync shots.
- Finalize audio timing before animation.
- Use fewer micro-cuts and smoother transitions.
- Lock character models, props, mirrors, and backgrounds more strictly.
- Tighten repeated teaching beats while preserving age-appropriate reinforcement.
