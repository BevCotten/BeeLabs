# Episode 001 — Limi’s First Haircut

## Episode Summary

Limi insists he is not nervous about his first haircut, even though his wiggly tummy and tight shoulders tell a different story. With help from Jazz, Maya, Limi Sr., and a patient barber named Ms. Toni, Limi learns what to expect, communicates what his body needs, and discovers that bravery does not mean never feeling nervous.

## Learning Goals

Children will learn how to:

- Name the feeling **nervous**
- Prepare for an unfamiliar experience
- Notice emotional clues in their bodies
- Ask questions when they feel unsure
- Tell a trusted adult when they need a break
- Use slow breathing to calm their bodies
- Understand that bravery means trying safely, even when something feels new

## Early-Learning Concepts

- Counting from 1 to 5
- Identifying basic shapes
- Recognizing loud and quiet sounds
- Following a sequence: **first, next, then, last**

## Target Length

Approximately **8–9 minutes**

---

## Scene 1 — “I’m Not Nervous!”

**Setting:** Limi’s bedroom, morning

Limi dances in front of his mirror while choosing between two pairs of fresh sneakers.

Maya enters the room.

**Maya:** “Good morning, Limi! After breakfast, we’re going to the barbershop.”

Limi suddenly stops dancing. His eyes widen, but he quickly straightens his shoulders.

**Limi:** “Okay! I’m not nervous.”

Jazz appears in the doorway and notices Limi squeezing both sneakers tightly against his chest.

**Jazz:** “You’re holding those shoes pretty tight for somebody who isn’t nervous.”

**Limi:** “I’m not nervous. My tummy is just… wiggly.”

Jazz kneels beside him.

**Jazz:** “Sometimes a wiggly tummy is our body’s way of saying we feel nervous.”

Limi looks toward the audience.

**Limi:** “Do I look nervous?”

### Audience Interaction

Pause so children can study Limi’s expression.

**Jazz:** “Let’s look for clues. Are his eyes wide? Are his shoulders tight? Is he squeezing his shoes?”

Limi slowly nods.

**Limi:** “Maybe I’m a little nervous.”

**Jazz:** “That’s okay. You can feel nervous and still learn what happens next.”

---

## Scene 2 — Practice with Mr. Curls

**Setting:** Limi’s bedroom

Jazz brings over Limi’s stuffed bear, **Mr. Curls**, and sits him on a small stool.

**Jazz:** “Let’s show Mr. Curls what happens at the barbershop.”

Limi moves closer.

Jazz gently places a small towel around the bear.

**Jazz:** “First, the barber puts on a cape to keep the tiny hairs off your clothes.”

**Limi:** “First—the cape!”

Jazz pretends to mist the bear’s hair.

**Jazz:** “Next, the barber gets your hair ready.”

**Limi:** “Next—get ready!”

Jazz holds up pretend toy clippers and makes a quiet buzzing sound.

**Jazz:** “Then, you may hear the clippers go, ‘Bzzzzzz.’”

Limi jumps slightly.

**Jazz:** “Was that sound loud or quiet?”

### Audience Interaction

Pause for children to answer.

**Limi:** “It was quiet. But the real clippers might sound louder.”

**Jazz:** “That’s right. If the sound or feeling becomes too much, you can say, ‘Break, please.’”

Limi practices with Mr. Curls.

**Limi:** “Break, please!”

**Jazz:** “Last, the barber brushes away the loose hair and shows you your fresh haircut.”

Limi turns Mr. Curls toward the mirror.

**Limi:** “Looking fresh, Mr. Curls!”

### Sequence Review

Jazz and Limi repeat the steps with the audience:

1. **First:** Put on the cape
2. **Next:** Get the hair ready
3. **Then:** Hear the clippers
4. **Last:** See the finished haircut

Limi places his sneakers beside him.

**Limi:** “Okay. I know what happens next.”

---

## Scene 2B — Dad Understands

**Setting:** Family kitchen during breakfast

Limi sits at the table while Maya prepares his plate. Jazz places Mr. Curls in the empty chair beside him.

Limi Sr. notices Limi looking thoughtful.

**Limi Sr.:** “How are you feeling about your haircut, Little Limi?”

**Limi:** “I know what happens now…but my tummy is still a little wiggly.”

Limi Sr. sits beside him.

**Limi Sr.:** “I understand. I feel nervous sometimes too.”

Limi looks surprised.

**Limi:** “Grown-ups get nervous?”

**Limi Sr.:** “We sure do. Feeling nervous tells me I may need more information, a slow breath, or someone I trust beside me.”

Limi Sr. places one hand gently over his stomach.

**Limi Sr.:** “Want to practice one breath together?”

### Audience Interaction

Limi Sr., Limi, and the audience breathe slowly:

- Smell the flower
- Blow out the candle

**Limi:** “Are you coming to the barbershop too?”

**Limi Sr.:** “Absolutely. We’re your family team.”

Maya and Jazz join their hands in the center of the table. Limi Sr. adds his hand, and Limi places his hand on top.

**Family:** “Team Limi!”

Limi smiles.

**Limi:** “Okay. Let’s go.”

---

## Guest Character — Ms. Toni

Ms. Toni is an older Black woman and an experienced neighborhood barber. She has a masculine-presenting style, a calm voice, and a warm sense of humor.

She wears fresh button-down shirts, clean sneakers, a barber apron, and a silver watch. She is confident, patient, and especially skilled at helping children feel safe during haircuts.

Ms. Toni explains what she is about to do, listens when a child says “stop” or “break,” and never makes fun of fear.

---

## Scene 3 — Welcome to Ms. Toni’s Chair

**Setting:** A warm neighborhood barbershop

The door chimes as Maya, Limi Sr., Jazz, and Limi enter.

Limi notices the mirrors, spinning barber pole, chairs, combs, and people talking. Clippers buzz in the background.

Limi reaches for Jazz’s hand.

Ms. Toni walks over with a gentle smile.

**Ms. Toni:** “Well, look who came to light up my shop!”

Limi gives a small smile.

**Ms. Toni:** “I’m Ms. Toni. Would you like a high five, a fist bump, or a wave?”

Limi thinks, then offers his fist.

**Limi:** “Fist bump.”

Ms. Toni gently bumps his fist.

**Ms. Toni:** “Good choice. In my chair, I tell you what I’m doing before I do it. If you need a break, what can you say?”

Limi looks toward the audience.

### Audience Interaction

Encourage children to say:

> **“Break, please!”**

**Limi:** “Break, please!”

**Ms. Toni:** “Exactly. Your words matter in my chair.”

Ms. Toni points to three shapes around the shop: a round mirror, a square floor tile, and a rectangular product box.

**Ms. Toni:** “Before we begin, can you help me find three shapes?”

Limi and the audience identify the **circle**, **square**, and **rectangle**.

**Limi:** “I found them all!”

**Ms. Toni:** “Then I think you’re ready to climb into the chair.”

---

## Scene 4 — One Challenge at a Time

**Setting:** Ms. Toni’s barber chair

Limi climbs into the chair. Ms. Toni raises it slowly.

**Ms. Toni:** “Up we go!”

Limi giggles as Maya fastens the cape around him.

### Challenge One — The Loud Clippers

Ms. Toni shows Limi the clippers while they are turned off.

**Ms. Toni:** “These are my clippers. Would you like to hear them from far away first?”

Limi nods.

Ms. Toni steps back and switches them on.

**Clippers:** “BZZZZZZZ!”

Limi’s shoulders rise toward his ears.

**Limi:** “That’s loud!”

Limi Sr. moves where Limi can clearly see him and places a calming hand over his own stomach.

**Jazz:** “Let’s help your body relax. Smell the flower…”

Limi Sr., Jazz, Limi, and the audience slowly breathe in.

**Jazz:** “…and blow out the candle.”

They slowly breathe out together.

### Audience Interaction

Invite children to breathe with Limi two times.

**Ms. Toni:** “Would you like to feel the vibration on your hand before I bring the clippers near your head?”

Limi holds out his hand. Ms. Toni gently touches the handle of the clippers to his palm.

**Limi:** “It tickles!”

The audience and Limi count together:

> “One, two, three, four, five!”

Ms. Toni completes the first small section.

**Ms. Toni:** “You did it. One part at a time.”

### Challenge Two — The Itchy Hair

A few tiny hairs land near Limi’s neck. He wiggles in the chair.

**Limi:** “It’s itchy!”

**Ms. Toni:** “Thank you for telling me. What do you need?”

Limi remembers his words.

**Limi:** “Break, please!”

Ms. Toni immediately turns off the clippers.

She uses a soft brush and towel to remove the loose hair.

**Ms. Toni:** “Does that feel better?”

**Limi:** “Much better.”

**Jazz:** “You noticed what your body needed, and you used your words.”

### Challenge Three — Sitting Still

Ms. Toni begins the final section, but Limi’s feet start swinging.

**Limi:** “My body wants to move!”

**Ms. Toni:** “Bodies need movement sometimes. Let’s take a wiggle break.”

Limi safely steps down with help. He wiggles his arms, stomps gently three times, and reaches toward the ceiling.

### Audience Interaction

Invite children to join:

- Wiggle both arms
- Stomp **three** times
- Reach up high
- Take one slow breath

Limi returns to the chair.

**Ms. Toni:** “For the last part, let’s play the Still-as-a-Statue Counting Game.”

Limi sits tall while everyone counts slowly from one to five.

> “One…two…three…four…five!”

Ms. Toni turns off the clippers.

**Ms. Toni:** “All finished.”

Limi opens his eyes wide.

**Limi:** “Already?”

---

## Scene 5 — Fresh, Proud, and Still Limi

Ms. Toni brushes away the last loose hairs and turns Limi toward the mirror.

His 4B curls are still soft and full, with a clean shape-up around the edges.

Limi studies his reflection. His eyes brighten, and a huge smile spreads across his face.

**Limi:** “That’s me! I look fresh!”

Ms. Toni holds up her fist.

**Ms. Toni:** “You sure do.”

Limi gives her a fist bump.

**Maya:** “You felt nervous, asked questions, took a break, and tried again.”

**Limi Sr.:** “And I watched you use your words and listen to your body. I’m proud of you.”

**Limi:** “So being brave doesn’t mean I can’t be nervous?”

**Jazz:** “Exactly. Brave means you listen to your feelings and keep trying safely.”

Limi turns toward the audience.

**Limi:** “Were you nervous with me?”

Pause for children to answer.

**Limi:** “What helped us?”

### Audience Review

Limi and Jazz review the coping tools:

- Take a slow breath
- Ask what will happen next
- Say, “Break, please”
- Move your body safely
- Try one small step at a time

Ms. Toni points toward the light symbol on Limi’s purple shirt.

**Ms. Toni:** “You learned something new today.”

Limi raises one finger in the air.

**Limi:** “Then let’s light it up!”

A soft lime glow shines around the light symbol as Limi does a happy dance.

## Closing Sequence Review

Limi reviews the haircut sequence with the audience:

1. **First:** Put on the cape
2. **Next:** Get the hair ready
3. **Then:** Use the clippers and take breaks when needed
4. **Last:** Look in the mirror

**Limi and the audience:**

> **“We learned something new—and now we shine!”**

Limi, Jazz, Maya, Limi Sr., and Ms. Toni dance together as the episode ends.
