# Episode 003: Limi’s Turn Is Coming

**Main lesson:** Waiting can feel hard. We can ask for a turn, get support, and find something playful to do while we wait.

**Repeated phrase:** “You go, then I go!”

### 1. A goodbye and a park adventure
Maya leaves for work. Limi wishes she could come, but gets excited when Limi Sr. and Jazz help him gather his things for the park.

### 2. New faces at the slide
Limi spots the slide and rushes toward it, with Dad close by. Two children, Mei and Jordan, are already taking turns.

### 3. “I want to go now!”
Limi wants to climb ahead. Dad gently stops him and explains who goes next. Limi stamps his foot. Dad stays calm: “You really want a turn. Waiting feels hard.”

### 4. A little help while waiting
Dad helps Limi notice the order: Mei, Jordan, then Limi. Jazz asks what they can pretend to be when they slide. Mei suggests rockets. Jordan watches with interest.

### 5. Friends ready for launch
Mei invites Limi to help count down. Jordan adds a funny rocket sound, and Limi laughs. They begin cheering for each other: “Three, two, one—whoosh!”

### 6. Limi’s turn
Limi climbs and slides, with Dad nearby. When he returns for another turn, he starts toward the ladder again—then pauses with a little reminder. “Jordan’s turn. Then me!”

### 7. Playing together
Mei has trouble leaving the slide entrance when her turn ends. The children get gentle help and practice again. Everyone is still learning.

### 8. Something to tell Maya
Later at home, Limi tells Maya, “I met Mei and Jordan! We played rockets!” Dad helps him remember how hard the wait felt—and how he found a way through it.

Limi invites the audience to practice: “You go, then I go!”

**Closing celebration:** “We learned something new—and now we shine!”

**Future connection:** Mei and Jordan can return in the preschool episode. Their reunion gives Limi a familiar connection while he adjusts to a new place.
