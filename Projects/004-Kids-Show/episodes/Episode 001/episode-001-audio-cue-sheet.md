# Episode 001 — Audio Cue Sheet

## Series

*Adventures with Limi*

## Purpose

This sheet tracks the remaining group dialogue, audience-response pauses, music, ambience, and sound effects for “Limi’s First Haircut.” All sounds should remain gentle, clear, and appropriate for children ages 2–4.

## Audio Safety and Style

- Dialogue always remains louder and clearer than music or sound effects.
- Clippers should sound realistic but never harsh, startling, or painfully loud.
- Sudden effects should use soft entrances and controlled volume.
- Audience pauses should last approximately three to five seconds.
- Use original recordings or sounds with documented permission for commercial use.
- Keep clean source files in WAV format whenever possible.

## Scene-by-Scene Sound Cues

### Scene 1 — Choosing Sneakers

- Soft morning room tone
- Gentle sneaker movement on the floor
- Light playful instrumental underscore
- Music stops softly when the haircut is announced

### Scene 2 — The Haircut Announcement

- Brief quiet beat after “haircut” to emphasize Limi’s reaction
- Subtle emotional underscore; avoid ominous or frightening music

### Scene 3 — Jazz Notices

- Warm, minimal underscore beneath the emotional conversation
- Small playful accent when Jazz raises her shoulders dramatically

### Scene 4 — Practicing With Tiggy

- Soft towel/cape movement
- Gentle pretend-clipper buzz created vocally by Limi
- Light chime for each sequence symbol: chair, cape, clippers, mirror

### Scene 5 — Team Limi at Breakfast

- Quiet kitchen ambience
- Soft plate and utensil movement
- Warm musical lift beneath “Team Limi!”

### Scene 6 — Arriving at the Barbershop

- Gentle outdoor neighborhood ambience
- Door opening and soft shop chime
- Low, welcoming barbershop room tone

### Scene 7 — Meeting Ms. Toni

- Soft fist-bump accent
- Quiet chair movement when Ms. Toni points toward it

### Scene 8 — Hearing the Clippers

- Clippers switch on from a safe distance
- Buzz grows only slightly as Ms. Toni moves closer
- Soft imaginative flower chime
- Gentle candle-whoosh on the exhale
- Clippers remain below all dialogue

### Scene 9 — “Break, Please”

- Clippers stop immediately after Limi asks for a break
- Soft towel or barber-brush movement
- Brief calm musical reassurance

### Scene 10 — Wiggle Break

- Upbeat movement instrumental
- Three soft, clear stomps synchronized with the count
- Music settles beneath the final slow breath

### Scene 11 — Still-as-a-Statue Game

- Very soft clipper buzz beneath the slow count
- Clippers switch off after “Five!”
- Small completion chime after “All finished!”

### Scene 12 — Mirror Reveal

- Gentle chair turn
- Warm musical rise before the reveal
- Soft sparkle accent as Limi sees his haircut

### Scene 13 — Light It Up

- Celebratory instrumental underscore
- Gentle electric-lime sparkle/glow sound
- Short instrumental reprise of the “Adventures with Limi” theme over the closing title
