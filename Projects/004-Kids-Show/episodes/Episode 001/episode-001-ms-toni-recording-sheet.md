# Episode 001 — Ms. Toni Voice Recording Sheet

## Series

*Adventures with Limi*

## Voice Direction

Ms. Toni is an older, masculine-presenting Black woman with a calm, comforting, experienced presence. For this temporary voice, use a lower and more grounded version of Bee’s natural voice.

- Lower the pitch slightly without forcing or straining the throat.
- Speak slowly, clearly, and confidently.
- Keep the tone warm and direct rather than overly sweet.
- Let gentle humor appear naturally in playful moments.
- Ms. Toni always respects Limi’s choices and responds immediately when he communicates a need.
- Record one scene at a time and leave about two seconds between lines.
- Keep AutoPitch off for spoken dialogue.

## Scene 7 — Meeting Ms. Toni

**Direction:** Welcoming and gently playful. Ms. Toni lowers herself to Limi’s height and gives him control over the greeting.

1. “Well, hello there! You must be Limi.”
2. “It’s very nice to meet both of you.”
3. “Would you like a fist bump, a wave, or just a smile today?”
4. “Boom! Great choice.”
5. “You can always tell me what feels comfortable for you.”
6. “Would you like to see the chair?”

## Scene 8 — Hearing the Clippers

**Direction:** Calm, predictable, and patient. Explain each step without rushing Limi.

1. “You know the steps!”
2. “These are my clippers. Would you like to hear them from over here first?”
3. “Are you ready for me to bring the clippers a little closer?”

## Scene 9 — “Break, Please”

**Direction:** Stop immediately and respond with sincere respect. Limi should hear that using his words was the right choice.

1. “Of course. Thank you for telling me what your body needs.”
2. “Then it’s time for a wiggle break.”

## Scene 10 — Wiggle Break

**Direction:** Patient and encouraging. The choice to continue belongs to Limi.

1. “Would you like to finish your haircut?”

## Scene 11 — Still-as-a-Statue Game

**Direction:** Clear and steady during the safety instruction, then warmly celebratory.

1. “For this part, I need your head to stay very still.”
2. “All finished!”
3. “You did it one small step at a time.”

## Scene 12 — Mirror Reveal

**Direction:** Build gentle anticipation, then affirm Limi’s courage with sincerity.

1. “Are you ready to see?”
2. “That is what courage looks like.”

## Scene 13 — Light It Up

**Direction:** Proud and celebratory, then clear and rhythmic for the lesson recap.

1. “And you tried something new.”
2. “And I can take things one step at a time.”
