# Episode 001 — Limi’s First Haircut

## Series Information

**Series:** *Adventures with Limi*  
**Episode:** 001  
**Audience:** Ages 2–4  
**Estimated Runtime:** 8–10 minutes  
**Theme Song:** “Adventures with Limi”

## Episode Summary

Limi feels excited about choosing his freshest sneakers—until Maya announces that it is haircut day. With help from Jazz, Maya, Limi Sr., Ms. Toni, and his stuffed tiger Tiggy, Limi learns what to expect at the barbershop. He recognizes nervous feelings in his body, practices calming strategies, asks for a break when he feels itchy, and discovers that courage means trying something new even when he feels nervous.

## Learning Goals

Children will learn how to:

- Recognize nervous feelings in the body
- Prepare for an unfamiliar experience
- Follow a simple sequence: first, next, then, last
- Use slow breathing and movement to calm their bodies
- Tell a trusted adult when they need a break
- Understand that they can feel nervous and brave at the same time

## Early-Learning Concepts

- Counting from one to five
- Sequencing everyday events
- Recognizing loud and quiet sounds
- Identifying physical clues connected to emotions
- Practicing clear, respectful self-advocacy

## Featured Characters

- **Limi** — a curious toddler preparing for his first haircut
- **Jazz** — Limi’s observant, protective teenage sister
- **Maya** — Limi’s warm and reassuring mother
- **Limi Sr.** — Limi’s practical, encouraging father
- **Ms. Toni** — a calm, experienced neighborhood barber
- **Tiggy** — Limi’s beloved stuffed tiger and comfort companion

## Episode Breakdown

### Scene 1 — Choosing Sneakers

Limi dances in front of his bedroom mirror while comparing purple and electric-lime sneakers. He invites the audience to help him choose. The playful morning establishes his confidence before he learns that something unfamiliar is coming.

### Scene 2 — The Haircut Announcement

Maya tells Limi that they are visiting Ms. Toni for his haircut after breakfast. Limi freezes, squeezes his sneakers, and insists that he is not nervous. Maya reassures him that his family will be with him.

### Scene 3 — Jazz Notices

Jazz observes Limi’s wide eyes, raised shoulders, tight grip, and wiggly tummy. She helps him name the feeling as nervousness and explains that he can be nervous and brave at the same time.

### Scene 4 — Practicing With Tiggy

Jazz and Limi use Tiggy to rehearse the haircut sequence through pretend play:

1. First, sit in the chair.
2. Next, put on the cape.
3. Then, hear the clippers.
4. Last, look in the mirror.

Knowing what to expect helps Limi feel more prepared.

### Scene 5 — Team Limi at Breakfast

At breakfast, Limi admits that the clippers may sound too loud. His family reminds him that he can listen from a distance, ask Ms. Toni to stop, and say, “Break, please.” They stack their hands and cheer, “Team Limi!”

### Scene 6 — Arriving at the Barbershop

Limi approaches the barbershop holding Tiggy and Limi Sr.’s hand. His family gives him time to look inside. He recognizes the chair and mirror from his practice and decides when he is ready to enter.

### Scene 7 — Meeting Ms. Toni

Ms. Toni greets Limi at his height and lets him choose between a fist bump, a wave, or a smile. Limi chooses a fist bump and learns that he can communicate what feels comfortable.

### Scene 8 — Hearing the Clippers

Ms. Toni shows Limi the clippers while they are off, then turns them on from a safe distance. When the buzzing feels loud, Jazz leads Limi and the audience through “smell the flower, blow out the candle.” Limi relaxes and asks Ms. Toni to bring the clippers a little closer.

### Scene 9 — “Break, Please”

Loose hair makes Limi’s neck itchy. He raises his hand and says, “Break, please.” Ms. Toni stops immediately and brushes away the hair. His family celebrates that he listened to his body and used his words.

### Scene 10 — Wiggle Break

Limi recognizes that his body needs movement. He, his family, and the audience wiggle, stomp three times, reach high, and take a slow breath. After checking how his body feels, Limi decides that he is ready to continue.

### Scene 11 — Still-as-a-Statue Game

Limi returns to the chair and plays a counting game while Ms. Toni completes the final edge. Everyone slowly counts from one to five as Limi practices body control.

### Scene 12 — Mirror Reveal

Ms. Toni turns Limi toward the mirror. He sees his healthy curls and fresh shape-up, then proudly says that he was nervous and still did it. Ms. Toni identifies his effort as courage.

### Scene 13 — Light It Up

Limi reviews the haircut sequence and remembers how he asked for a break. Each family member names one helpful strategy. Limi’s shirt symbol glows as everyone celebrates with the closing phrase:

> **“We learned something new—and now we shine!”**

An instrumental reprise of the “Adventures with Limi” theme plays over the closing title card.

## Core Takeaway

Children do not have to stop feeling nervous before they try something new. Preparation, trusted support, calming strategies, movement, and clear words can help them move forward one small step at a time.
