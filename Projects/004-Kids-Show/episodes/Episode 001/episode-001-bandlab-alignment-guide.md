# Episode 001 BandLab Alignment Guide

## Adventures with Limi — “Limi’s First Haircut”

Use this sheet to arrange the dialogue after the 21-second opening theme. The times are intentionally left open because the natural performances and toddler-response pauses will determine the final runtime.

## Timing Rules

- Start Scene 1 at approximately `00:22.0`.
- Leave about 0.5–1 second between normal lines.
- Leave 2–3 seconds at every audience-response marker.
- Leave 2–3 seconds for each missing short Jazz line and 4–6 seconds for her longer lines.
- Keep every character on their own BandLab track.
- Start the barbershop ambience in Scene 6 and cntinue it quietly through Scene 13.

---

## Opening Title Sequence

1. **THEME SONG:** Play the complete “Adventures with Limi” theme.
2. Allow approximately 1 second of space after the final beat.
3. Begin Scene 1.
