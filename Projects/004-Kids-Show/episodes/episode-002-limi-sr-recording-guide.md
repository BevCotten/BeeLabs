# Episode 002 — Limi Sr. Recording Guide

**Episode:** *Limi’s First Dental Visit*  
**Minimum:** Record **at least two takes of every dialogue line**.

## Performance Guide
- Calm, loving father.
- Grounded and encouraging.
- Give Limi confidence without taking over the moment.
- Keep this voice distinct from Dr. Miles: Limi Sr. is the familiar dad voice; Dr. Miles is softer, slower, and more professionally gentle.
- Leave about two seconds between lines.

## Scene 3 — Limi Has Questions
**Direction:** Calm and matter-of-fact. Explain what Limi will see, then reassure him that he never has to pretend to understand.
1. “You’ll see a special chair and a bright light so the dentist can see your teeth.”
2. “You don’t have to pretend you understand.”

## Scene 13 — Sparkly Smile, Light It Up
**Direction:** Proud, grounded affirmation. Emphasize that Limi tried when *he* felt ready.
1. “And you tried when you felt ready.”
2. “And I can try when I’m ready.”
