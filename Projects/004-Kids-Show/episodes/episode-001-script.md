# Episode 001 Script — Limi’s First Haircut

## Episode Information

**Series:** *Limi Lights Up!*
**Episode:** 001
**Title:** “Limi’s First Haircut”
**Audience:** Ages 2–4
**Estimated Runtime:** 8–10 minutes

## Learning Goals

* Recognize nervous feelings in the body
* Learn what happens during a haircut
* Practice sequencing: first, next, then, last
* Use calming breaths and movement breaks
* Ask for a break when something feels uncomfortable
* Build confidence when trying something new

## Featured Characters

* **Limi**
* **Jazz**
* **Maya**
* **Limi Sr.**
* **Ms. Toni**
* **Mr. Curls**, Limi’s stuffed tiger

---

## Scene 1 — Choosing Sneakers

**Location:** Limi’s bedroom
**Storyboard Panel:** 1

Morning sunlight fills Limi’s colorful bedroom. Limi stands in front of his mirror holding two pairs of sneakers.

One pair has bright purple details. The other has electric-lime details.

Music begins to play.

**LIMI**
Purple shoes?

Limi holds the purple sneakers against his feet and strikes a pose.

**LIMI**
Ooooh!

He holds up the lime sneakers.

**LIMI**
Or green shoes?

Limi dances from side to side while comparing them.

**LIMI**
Purple…green…purple…green!

He looks toward the audience.

**LIMI**
Which pair would you choose?

**PAUSE FOR AUDIENCE RESPONSE**

Limi examines both pairs very seriously.

**LIMI**
Hmm…I choose—

Maya appears in the doorway.

---

## Scene 2 — The Haircut Announcement

**Location:** Limi’s bedroom
**Storyboard Panel:** 2

**MAYA**
Good morning, my little light!

**LIMI**
Good morning, Mommy!

**MAYA**
Pick your favorite sneakers. After breakfast, we’re going to see Ms. Toni for your haircut.

The music stops.

Limi freezes while clutching both pairs of sneakers.

His eyes grow wide. His shoulders rise toward his ears.

**LIMI**
My…haircut?

**MAYA**
That’s right. Ms. Toni is going to make your curls look fresh.

Limi forces a tiny smile.

**LIMI**
I’m not nervous.

His fingers squeeze the sneakers tightly.

Maya gives him a gentle, understanding smile.

**MAYA**
Okay. We’ll all be there with you.

Maya leaves to prepare breakfast.

Jazz walks past the doorway, then stops when she notices Limi.

---

## Scene 3 — Jazz Notices

**Location:** Limi’s bedroom
**Storyboard Panel:** 3

Jazz kneels beside Limi.

**JAZZ**
Hmm. Your eyes are wide.

Limi blinks.

**JAZZ**
Your shoulders are way up here.

Jazz raises her shoulders dramatically.

**JAZZ**
And you’re squeezing those shoes very tightly.

Limi looks down at his hands.

**JAZZ**
Sometimes our bodies tell us how we feel before our words do.

**LIMI**
My tummy feels wiggly.

**JAZZ**
That sounds like nervous feelings.

**LIMI**
But I’m brave!

**JAZZ**
You can be nervous and brave at the same time.

Limi thinks about this.

**LIMI**
Nervous…and brave?

**JAZZ**
Exactly. Being brave means trying even when something feels new.

Jazz notices Mr. Curls, Limi’s stuffed tiger, sitting on the bed.

**JAZZ**
I have an idea. Let’s show Mr. Curls what happens during a haircut.

Limi smiles.

**LIMI**
Mr. Curls needs a fresh cut!

---

## Scene 4 — Practicing With Mr. Curls

**Location:** Limi’s bedroom floor
**Storyboard Panel:** 4

Jazz and Limi sit on the floor with Mr. Curls between them.

Jazz wraps a small towel around the stuffed tiger like a barber cape. Limi holds pretend clippers.

**JAZZ**
Haircuts happen one step at a time.

Four colorful symbols appear:

1. A chair
2. A cape
3. Clippers
4. A mirror

**JAZZ**
First, you sit in the chair.

Limi places Mr. Curls on a small chair.

**LIMI**
Sit in the chair!

**JAZZ**
Next, the barber puts on the cape.

They fasten the pretend cape around Mr. Curls.

**LIMI**
Cape on!

**JAZZ**
Then, you hear the clippers.

Limi moves the pretend clippers near Mr. Curls.

**LIMI**
Buzzzzzzz!

They giggle.

**JAZZ**
Last, you look in the mirror.

Limi holds a small mirror in front of Mr. Curls.

**LIMI**
Looking fresh, Mr. Curls!

Jazz turns toward the audience.

**JAZZ**
Can you remember the steps with us?

**JAZZ AND LIMI**
First—the chair!
Next—the cape!
Then—the clippers!
Last—the mirror!

**JAZZ**
See? Now we know what to expect.

**LIMI**
Can Mr. Curls come too?

**JAZZ**
Absolutely.

Limi hugs his stuffed tiger.

---

## Scene 5 — Team Limi at Breakfast

**Location:** Family kitchen
**Storyboard Panel:** 5

The family sits together at the breakfast table. Mr. Curls sits safely nearby.

Limi pokes at his fruit.

Limi Sr. notices.

**LIMI SR.**
You’ve got something on your mind, little man.

**LIMI**
The clippers might sound too loud.

**LIMI SR.**
They might sound loud at first. But you can listen from far away before they come close.

**MAYA**
You can also tell Ms. Toni if you need her to stop.

**JAZZ**
And you already know how to ask for a break.

**LIMI**
I can say, “Break, please!”

**LIMI SR.**
That’s right. Using your words is a strong thing to do.

Limi Sr. places his hand in the center of the table.

**LIMI SR.**
Team Limi?

Maya places her hand on his. Jazz adds hers.

Limi looks at his family and puts his small hand on top.

**EVERYONE**
Team Limi!

They lift their hands together.

**LIMI**
Let’s light it up!

---

## Scene 6 — Arriving at the Barbershop

**Location:** Outside and entrance of Ms. Toni’s barbershop
**Storyboard Panel:** 6

The family approaches the warm, welcoming barbershop.

Limi holds Mr. Curls in one arm and Limi Sr.’s hand with the other.

Limi slows down at the entrance.

From inside, they hear the gentle sounds of conversation and barber tools.

**MAYA**
We’re here.

**LIMI SR.**
There’s no rush. We can walk inside when you’re ready.

Limi looks through the door.

**JAZZ**
What do you notice?

**LIMI**
A big chair.

**MAYA**
Just like the one we practiced with.

**LIMI**
And a mirror!

**JAZZ**
That’s the last step!

Limi takes a slow breath.

**LIMI**
I’m ready to go inside.

The family enters together.

---

## Scene 7 — Meeting Ms. Toni

**Location:** Inside the barbershop
**Storyboard Panel:** 7

Ms. Toni approaches with a warm smile. She lowers herself closer to Limi’s height.

**MS. TONI**
Well, hello there! You must be Limi.

Limi holds Mr. Curls close.

**LIMI**
This is Mr. Curls.

**MS. TONI**
It’s very nice to meet both of you.

Ms. Toni gently offers Limi a fist bump.

**MS. TONI**
Would you like a fist bump, a wave, or just a smile today?

Limi thinks.

He looks toward the audience.

**LIMI**
Which greeting should I choose?

**PAUSE FOR AUDIENCE RESPONSE**

Limi smiles and gives Ms. Toni a fist bump.

**LIMI**
Fist bump!

**MS. TONI**
Boom! Great choice.

**JAZZ**
You got to choose how you wanted to say hello.

**MS. TONI**
You can always tell me what feels comfortable for you.

Ms. Toni points toward the barber chair.

**MS. TONI**
Would you like to see the chair?

Limi nods.

---

## Scene 8 — Hearing the Clippers

**Location:** Barber chair
**Storyboard Panel:** 8

Limi sits in the barber chair with Mr. Curls nearby.

Ms. Toni fastens the cape gently around him.

**LIMI**
First, the chair. Next, the cape.

**MS. TONI**
You know the steps!

Ms. Toni shows Limi the clippers while they are turned off.

**MS. TONI**
These are my clippers. Would you like to hear them from over here first?

Limi nods.

Ms. Toni steps a safe distance away and turns them on.

**SOUND:** Buzzzzzzz.

Limi’s shoulders rise.

**LIMI**
That’s loud!

Jazz stands where Limi can see her.

**JAZZ**
Let’s take our slow breath.

A gentle flower appears in Limi’s imagination.

**JAZZ**
Smell the flower.

Limi and Jazz breathe in slowly through their noses.

A glowing candle appears.

**JAZZ**
Now blow out the candle.

They slowly breathe out.

**JAZZ**
Can everyone help Limi?

**JAZZ AND LIMI**
Smell the flower…

**PAUSE TO BREATHE IN**

**JAZZ AND LIMI**
Blow out the candle.

**PAUSE TO BREATHE OUT**

Limi’s shoulders relax.

**MS. TONI**
Are you ready for me to bring the clippers a little closer?

**LIMI**
A little closer.

Ms. Toni moves slowly and explains what she is doing.

---

## Scene 9 — “Break, Please”

**Location:** Barber chair
**Storyboard Panel:** 9

Ms. Toni carefully trims Limi’s hair.

A few tiny hairs fall onto his cape and near his neck.

Limi wiggles in the chair.

**LIMI**
It feels itchy.

Ms. Toni pauses to listen.

Limi remembers what his family taught him. He raises his hand.

**LIMI**
Break, please!

Ms. Toni immediately turns off the clippers and lowers them safely.

**MS. TONI**
Of course. Thank you for telling me what your body needs.

Ms. Toni brushes the loose hairs away with a soft towel.

**MAYA**
You used your words.

**LIMI SR.**
That was strong.

**JAZZ**
What would help your body feel better?

Limi looks down at his wiggly feet.

**LIMI**
I need to move!

**MS. TONI**
Then it’s time for a wiggle break.

---

## Scene 10 — Wiggle Break

**Location:** Beside the barber chair
**Storyboard Panel:** 10

The clippers remain safely on the counter.

Limi steps down from the chair. The family gives him plenty of space.

Upbeat music begins.

**JAZZ**
Everybody stand up if you can!

**LIMI**
Wiggle, wiggle, wiggle!

Everyone wiggles.

**JAZZ**
Now stomp three times!

**EVERYONE**
One!

**STOMP**

**EVERYONE**
Two!

**STOMP**

**EVERYONE**
Three!

**STOMP**

**LIMI**
Reach up high!

Everyone stretches their hands toward the ceiling.

**MAYA**
And take one slow breath.

Everyone breathes in and out.

**LIMI SR.**
How does your body feel now?

Limi checks his shoulders, tummy, hands, and feet.

**LIMI**
My body feels ready.

**MS. TONI**
Would you like to finish your haircut?

**LIMI**
Yes, please!

---

## Scene 11 — Still-as-a-Statue Game

**Location:** Barber chair
**Storyboard Panel:** 11

Limi climbs back into the chair.

**MS. TONI**
For this part, I need your head to stay very still.

**JAZZ**
Let’s play the Still-as-a-Statue Game!

Limi sits tall and places his hands in his lap.

**LIMI**
I am a statue.

**JAZZ**
Can everyone help Limi count slowly to five?

**EVERYONE**
One…

Ms. Toni carefully shapes one side of Limi’s hair.

**EVERYONE**
Two…

Limi stays still.

**EVERYONE**
Three…

Limi’s nose twitches, but he keeps his head steady.

**EVERYONE**
Four…

Ms. Toni finishes the final edge.

**EVERYONE**
Five!

Ms. Toni switches off the clippers.

**MS. TONI**
All finished!

Limi’s eyes grow wide.

**LIMI**
Already?

**MS. TONI**
You did it one small step at a time.

---

## Scene 12 — Mirror Reveal

**Location:** Barber chair and mirror
**Storyboard Panel:** 12

Ms. Toni gently brushes the last loose hairs from Limi’s cape.

She turns the chair toward the large mirror.

**MS. TONI**
Are you ready to see?

**LIMI**
I’m ready!

Limi looks into the mirror.

His curls are full and healthy, and his shape-up is fresh and clean.

Limi touches one curl carefully.

**LIMI**
Whoa!

He leans closer to the mirror.

**LIMI**
That’s me?

**JAZZ**
That’s you, little bro!

**MAYA**
Look at that smile.

**LIMI SR.**
Looking fresh, son!

Limi grins proudly.

**LIMI**
I was nervous…and I did it!

**MS. TONI**
That is what courage looks like.

---

## Scene 13 — Light It Up

**Location:** Open area of the barbershop
**Storyboard Panel:** 13

Ms. Toni removes the cape.

Limi hops down wearing his purple-and-lime outfit and fresh sneakers.

The music returns.

**LIMI**
First, I sat in the chair!

Limi points toward the chair.

**LIMI**
Next, I wore the cape!

He pretends to wrap a cape around himself.

**LIMI**
Then, I heard the clippers!

He makes a buzzing sound.

**LIMI**
Buzzzzz!

**LIMI**
Last, I looked in the mirror!

He strikes a proud pose.

**JAZZ**
And what did you do when your neck felt itchy?

**LIMI**
I said, “Break, please!”

**MAYA**
You listened to your body.

**LIMI SR.**
You used your words.

**MS. TONI**
And you tried something new.

Limi turns toward the audience.

**LIMI**
Did you try the breathing and wiggle break with me?

**PAUSE FOR AUDIENCE RESPONSE**

**LIMI**
You did? We were brave together!

A gentle electric-lime glow shines from the symbol on Limi’s shirt. Tiny sparkling lights float around him.

Everyone begins dancing.

**LIMI**
When something feels new, I can breathe.

**JAZZ**
I can ask questions.

**MAYA**
I can listen to my body.

**LIMI SR.**
I can ask for help.

**MS. TONI**
And I can take things one step at a time.

Limi raises one hand proudly.

**LIMI**
We learned something new—

**EVERYONE**
—and now we shine!

The family laughs and continues dancing as the screen fills with a warm purple-and-lime glow.

## End of Episode
