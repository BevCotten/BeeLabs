# Episode 002 Recording Guide — Limi’s First Dental Visit

**Series:** *Adventures with Limi*  
**Episode:** 002  
**Recording rule:** Record **at least two takes of every dialogue line**.

## Performance Guide

- Keep every performance warm, natural, and easy for ages 2–4 to understand.
- Record one scene at a time.
- Leave about 2 seconds between lines.
- Record **Take 1 and Take 2 minimum** for every dialogue line.
- Do not record stage directions, audience pauses, silent B-roll, or sound effects as dialogue.
- Preserve the pauses shown with ellipses.
- Limi should sound like a real curious toddler, not an adult performing a “kid voice.”
- Maya is loving and reassuring.
- Limi Sr. is calm, grounded, and encouraging.
- Jazz has supportive, playful big-sister energy.
- Dr. Miles is warm, patient, playful, and unhurried—never clinical, stern, or patronizing.

---

## Scene 1 — Morning Brush

**Direction:** Playful family-morning energy. Maya is gently guiding Limi through brushing; Limi turns it into a lion game.

1. **MAYA:** “Brush, brush, brush.”
2. **MAYA:** “Front teeth...back teeth...and way in the back.”
3. **LIMI:** “Aaaaah!”
4. **LIMI:** “ROAR!”
5. **MAYA:** “That is one serious brushing lion.”
6. **MAYA:** “Little kids need a grown-up to help make sure every tooth gets clean.”
7. **LIMI:** “Roar!”

---

## Scene 2 — Dentist Day

**Direction:** Maya stays casual and positive. Limi is surprised and unsure—not frightened. His audience question should feel genuinely curious.

1. **MAYA (O.S.):** “And those teeth are going to be extra clean for Dr. Miles today.”
2. **LIMI:** “The dentist?”
3. **LIMI:** “Have you been to the dentist before?”
4. **LIMI:** “I have questions.”

---

## Scene 3 — Limi Has Questions

**Direction:** This is a calm family conversation. Limi is genuinely curious. Everyone answers simply and confidently so he feels safe asking questions.

1. **LIMI:** “What happens at the dentist?”
2. **MAYA:** “The dentist checks your teeth and helps keep your mouth healthy.”
3. **LIMI SR.:** “You’ll see a special chair and a bright light so the dentist can see your teeth.”
4. **JAZZ:** “And if you see something you don’t know, you can ask about it.”
5. **LIMI:** “What if I don’t know what something does?”
6. **MAYA:** “Then you ask.”
7. **LIMI SR.:** “You don’t have to pretend you understand.”
8. **JAZZ:** “Actually, I have a plan for new things.”

---

## Scene 4 — The New-Thing Plan

**Direction:** Jazz teaches the three steps slowly and clearly. Limi learns the rhythm, then becomes excited to teach the audience. Give “Ask. Look. Try.” plenty of space.

1. **JAZZ:** “First—ask.”
2. **JAZZ:** “Next—look.”
3. **JAZZ:** “Then—try when you’re ready.”
4. **LIMI:** “Ask...look...try.”
5. **JAZZ:** “Exactly.”
6. **JAZZ:** “Show me first...”
7. **LIMI:** “...then I can try!”
8. **JAZZ:** “That’s your new-thing plan.”
9. **LIMI:** “Can you do it with me?”
10. **LIMI:** “Ask!”
11. **LIMI:** “Look!”
12. **LIMI:** “Try!”
13. **LIMI:** “Show me first, then I can try!”
14. **LIMI:** “Come on, Tiggy. We have a plan.”

---

## Scene 5 — Arriving at the Dentist

**Direction:** **No dialogue to record.** This scene is silent B-roll with a light, curious instrumental transition.

---

## Scene 6 — Meeting Dr. Miles

**Direction:** Dr. Miles immediately establishes safety. James should use a softer, slower, gentler delivery than Limi Sr. Speak directly to Limi with warmth and respect.

1. **DR. MILES:** “Hi, Limi. I’m Dr. Miles.”
2. **DR. MILES:** “I check little teeth and care for big smiles.”
3. **DR. MILES:** “Before I do anything, I can tell you what it is and show you how it works.”

---

## Scene 7 — Fist Bump

**Direction:** Limi is becoming more comfortable and politely asks for the greeting he wants. Dr. Miles responds sincerely and affirmingly.

1. **LIMI:** “It’s nice to meet you, Dr. Miles. Can we do a fist bump?”
2. **DR. MILES:** “Thank you for telling me which greeting you’re comfortable with.”

---

## Scene 8 — Choosing the Chair

**Direction:** Keep this warm and reassuring. Dr. Miles presents choices without pressure. Maya sounds loving and close. Limi’s rocket-ship line should build from wonder into playful excitement.

1. **DR. MILES:** “This is the dental chair. You can climb in with Mom right beside you, or you can sit on Mom’s lap.”
2. **MAYA:** “You choose, baby. I’m right here with you.”
3. **LIMI:** “The chair.”
4. **MAYA:** “Okay, my love. I’ll be right beside you.”
5. **LIMI:** “I am on a rocket ship! One, two, three... blast off!”

---

## Scene 9 — The Bright Light

**Direction:** Dr. Miles acknowledges that the light can look scary without making the moment scary. He stays calm and matter-of-fact. Limi moves from uncertainty to delighted recognition and then confidence.

1. **DR. MILES:** “This light is really bright. I know it might look a little scary, but it doesn’t hurt. It just helps me see your smile.”
2. **DR. MILES:** “Would you like me to show you how it works before I use it?”
3. **LIMI:** “Yes, please.”
4. **DR. MILES:** “See? It makes a bright circle so I can see everything clearly.”
5. **LIMI:** “Ohhh! It’s like a flashlight!”
6. **DR. MILES:** “Exactly! Are you ready for me to shine it above you?”
7. **LIMI:** “I’m ready.”

---

## Scene 10 — The Tiny Mirror

**Direction:** Dr. Miles introduces the mirror simply and patiently. Limi studies it, remembers his new-thing plan, and makes his own decision to try.

1. **DR. MILES:** “This is a tiny mirror for looking at teeth.”
2. **DR. MILES:** “See? It’s a mirror that shows me different angles of your teeth.”
3. **LIMI:** “Tiny mirror.”
4. **DR. MILES:** “Would you like me to use it to look at your teeth?”
5. **LIMI:** “I looked...and now I can try.”
6. **DR. MILES:** “You got it.”

---

## Scene 11 — Count Limi’s Teeth

**Direction:** Dr. Miles is playful and encouraging. These lines are off-screen. Leave genuine space for children watching to count along.

1. **DR. MILES (O.S.):** “Can you open wide like a lion?”
2. **DR. MILES (O.S.):** “Whoa! That is a big lion mouth.”
3. **DR. MILES (O.S.):** “Can everybody help me count while I look?”
4. **DR. MILES (O.S.):** “One...two...three...four...”
5. **DR. MILES (O.S.):** “Keep counting!”
6. **DR. MILES (O.S.):** “Great job, Limi. You kept your mouth open while I counted.”

---

## Scene 12 — Show Me Again, Please

**Direction:** Limi is surprised by the sound but does not panic. “Show me again, please” should be calm, clear self-advocacy. Dr. Miles stops immediately and explains without pressure. Limi’s final three lines should sound deliberate and increasingly confident.

1. **LIMI:** “Show me again, please.”
2. **DR. MILES:** “This little straw takes away extra water.”
3. **DR. MILES:** “Would you like to try now?”
4. **LIMI:** “I asked.”
5. **LIMI:** “I looked.”
6. **LIMI:** “Now I can try.”

---

## Scene 13 — Sparkly Smile, Light It Up

**Direction:** This is the emotional payoff. Limi is proud and excited, while the adults reinforce exactly what he did well. Build naturally into the family lesson and joyful final line without shouting.

1. **LIMI:** “Ooooh!”
2. **LIMI:** “My teeth are shiny!”
3. **DR. MILES:** “And you did a great job asking questions.”
4. **JAZZ:** “What was your new-thing plan?”
5. **LIMI:** “I asked...”
6. **LIMI:** “I looked...”
7. **LIMI:** “And then I tried!”
8. **LIMI:** “Did you practice with me?”
9. **LIMI:** “We did it!”
10. **MAYA:** “You asked when you wanted to understand something.”
11. **LIMI SR.:** “And you tried when you felt ready.”
12. **DR. MILES:** “That is a great way to learn about something new.”
13. **LIMI:** “When something is new—”
14. **JAZZ:** “I can ask.”
15. **MAYA:** “I can look.”
16. **LIMI SR.:** “And I can try when I’m ready.”
17. **LIMI:** “Show me first, then I can try!”
18. **LIMI:** “We learned something new—”
19. **EVERYONE:** “—and now we shine!”
