# Episode 002 — Maya Recording Guide

**Episode:** *Limi’s First Dental Visit*  
**Minimum:** Record **at least two takes of every dialogue line**.

## Performance Guide
- Loving, warm mother.
- Reassuring without sounding worried or overly protective.
- Her calm presence tells Limi he is safe while still allowing him to make choices.
- Keep the delivery conversational and clear for ages 2–4.
- Leave about two seconds between lines.

## Scene 1 — Morning Brush
**Direction:** Warm family-morning energy. Gently guide the brushing while enjoying Limi’s lion game.
1. “Brush, brush, brush.”
2. “Front teeth...back teeth...and way in the back.”
3. “That is one serious brushing lion.”
4. “Little kids need a grown-up to help make sure every tooth gets clean.”

## Scene 2 — Dentist Day
**Direction:** Off-screen, upbeat and casual. Do not make the dentist announcement sound ominous.
1. “And those teeth are going to be extra clean for Dr. Miles today.”

## Scene 3 — Limi Has Questions
**Direction:** Simple, confident reassurance. Treat his questions as completely normal.
1. “The dentist checks your teeth and helps keep your mouth healthy.”
2. “Then you ask.”

## Scene 8 — Choosing the Chair
**Direction:** Very warm mother-son moment. Let Limi know he has a choice and that you will stay close without sounding anxious.
1. “You choose, baby. I’m right here with you.”
2. “Okay, my love. I’ll be right beside you.”

## Scene 13 — Sparkly Smile, Light It Up
**Direction:** Proud and affectionate. Praise the specific skill he used, then join the family lesson naturally.
1. “You asked when you wanted to understand something.”
2. “I can look.”
