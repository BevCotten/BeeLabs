# Episode 002 — Jazz Recording Guide

**Episode:** *Limi’s First Dental Visit*  
**Minimum:** Record **at least two takes of every dialogue line**.

## Performance Guide
- Supportive older-sister energy.
- Confident and playful, never lecturing.
- She makes the lesson easy for Limi and the audience to remember.
- Keep the key steps rhythmic and clear.
- Leave about two seconds between lines.

## Scene 3 — Limi Has Questions
**Direction:** Helpful big-sister energy. The second line should sound like she has just thought of a useful idea.
1. “And if you see something you don’t know, you can ask about it.”
2. “Actually, I have a plan for new things.”

## Scene 4 — The New-Thing Plan
**Direction:** Teach the three steps slowly and clearly. Give each key word its own beat. “Show me first...” should invite Limi to finish the phrase.
1. “First—ask.”
2. “Next—look.”
3. “Then—try when you’re ready.”
4. “Exactly.”
5. “Show me first...”
6. “That’s your new-thing plan.”

## Scene 13 — Sparkly Smile, Light It Up
**Direction:** Playful prompt first, then a clear family call-and-response.
1. “What was your new-thing plan?”
2. “I can ask.”
