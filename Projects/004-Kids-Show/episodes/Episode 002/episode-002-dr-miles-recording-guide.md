# Episode 002 — Dr. Miles Recording Guide

**Episode:** *Limi’s First Dental Visit*  
**Minimum:** Record **at least two takes of every dialogue line**.

## Performance Guide
- Warm, patient, playful pediatric dentist.
- James should use a softer, slower, gentler delivery than Limi Sr.
- Speak directly to Limi with respect; never clinical, stern, sing-song, or patronizing.
- Give a toddler time to process each explanation and question.
- Leave about two seconds between lines.

## Scene 6 — Meeting Dr. Miles
**Direction:** Establish safety immediately. Friendly, calm, and reassuring without overdoing it.
1. “Hi, Limi. I’m Dr. Miles.”
2. “I check little teeth and care for big smiles.”
3. “Before I do anything, I can tell you what it is and show you how it works.”

## Scene 7 — Fist Bump
**Direction:** Genuine affirmation. He respects that Limi chose his own greeting.
1. “Thank you for telling me which greeting you’re comfortable with.”

## Scene 8 — Choosing the Chair
**Direction:** Present both choices neutrally and without pressure.
1. “This is the dental chair. You can climb in with Mom right beside you, or you can sit on Mom’s lap.”

## Scene 9 — The Bright Light
**Direction:** Acknowledge that the light may look scary without making the moment frightening. Calm, matter-of-fact, and patient.
1. “This light is really bright. I know it might look a little scary, but it doesn’t hurt. It just helps me see your smile.”
2. “Would you like me to show you how it works before I use it?”
3. “See? It makes a bright circle so I can see everything clearly.”
4. “Exactly! Are you ready for me to shine it above you?”

## Scene 10 — The Tiny Mirror
**Direction:** Introduce the tool simply. Give Limi time to inspect it and decide when he is ready.
1. “This is a tiny mirror for looking at teeth.”
2. “See? It’s a mirror that shows me different angles of your teeth.”
3. “Would you like me to use it to look at your teeth?”
4. “You got it.”

## Scene 11 — Count Limi’s Teeth
**Direction:** Playful and encouraging. These lines are off-screen. Leave genuine space for children watching to count along.
1. “Can you open wide like a lion?”
2. “Whoa! That is a big lion mouth.”
3. “Can everybody help me count while I look?”
4. “One...two...three...four...”
5. “Keep counting!”
6. “Great job, Limi. You kept your mouth open while I counted.”

## Scene 12 — Show Me Again, Please
**Direction:** Limi has asked to see the tool again. Respond immediately and without pressure; explain it simply, then offer the choice to try.
1. “This little straw takes away extra water.”
2. “Would you like to try now?”

## Scene 13 — Sparkly Smile, Light It Up
**Direction:** Specific, sincere praise. Reinforce the skill Limi used rather than simply calling him brave.
1. “And you did a great job asking questions.”
2. “That is a great way to learn about something new.”
