# Episode 001 — Limi Sr. Voice Recording Sheet

## Series

*Adventures with Limi*

## Voice Direction

Limi Sr. has James’s natural Southern Black accent. His delivery should feel authentic, confident, warm, playful, and emotionally safe. The accent should never be exaggerated or treated as a joke.

- Speak slightly slower than normal for children ages 2–4.
- Keep important words clear while preserving natural rhythm and pronunciation.
- Sound like a loving father—not a narrator or generic cartoon character.
- Leave about two seconds between lines.
- Record one scene at a time.
- Keep AutoPitch off for spoken dialogue.

## Scene 5 — Team Limi at Breakfast

**Direction:** Begin observant and gentle. Build toward confident, playful family energy.

1. “You’ve got something on your mind, little man.”
2. “They might sound loud at first. But you can listen from far away before they come close.”
3. “That’s right. Using your words is a strong thing to do.”
4. “Team Limi?”

## Scene 6 — Arriving at the Barbershop

**Direction:** Patient and unhurried. Make it clear that Limi has time and support.

1. “There’s no rush. We can walk inside when you’re ready.”

## Scene 9 — “Break, Please”

**Direction:** Quiet pride. Keep the affirmation simple and sincere.

1. “That was strong.”

## Scene 10 — Wiggle Break

**Direction:** Calm and curious. Give Limi room to check in with his own body.

1. “How does your body feel now?”

## Scene 12 — Mirror Reveal

**Direction:** Proud, playful, and genuinely impressed.

1. “Looking fresh, son!”

## Scene 13 — Light It Up

**Direction:** Celebrate Limi first, then deliver the final lesson clearly and rhythmically.

1. “You used your words.”
2. “I can ask for help.”
