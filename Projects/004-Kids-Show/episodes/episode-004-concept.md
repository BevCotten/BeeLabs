# Episode 004 — Limi's Big Boy Bed

**Status:** Approved concept

**Series continuity:** Episode 003, [Limi’s Turn Is Coming](episode-003-outline.md), follows Limi’s first visit to the playground. This bedtime story follows as Episode 004.

**Premise:** Limi is excited about his new Montessori floor bed during the day and feels nervous when bedtime comes. Over four nights, Maya and Limi Sr. help him get comfortable in his own bed. They reassure him that they’re nearby and come when he calls for help.

**Central message:** “Even when Mommy and Daddy aren't in my room, I'm safe, I'm loved, and they're always nearby when I need them.”

**Recurring affirmation:** “My bed is my cozy place. Mommy and Daddy are nearby. If I need them, they will help me. And when morning comes, we'll be together again!”

## Four nights and the morning after

### Night 1 — The First Try

Limi loves his new bed during the day. At bedtime, he feels nervous. Maya sits beside him, holds his hand, and reassures him that she’s nearby. He falls asleep with her beside him.

### Night 2 — Daddy Comes Back

Limi wakes and calls for Daddy. Limi Sr. comes as promised and comforts him in his bed. Limi learns he can ask for help and settle again in his own cozy spot.

### Night 3 — A Little More Space

Maya reads Limi a bedtime story, hugs him, and moves to a nearby chair. Limi can still see her. When he gets nervous, she speaks gently to him, and he settles with her close by.

### Night 4 — I Can Try!

Maya and Limi Sr. share the familiar bedtime routine, give Limi hugs, and say goodnight. Limi tries settling in his bed with both parents just outside his room. They check on him when he needs reassurance, and he drifts off.

### The Next Morning — Look What I Did!

Limi wakes in his big boy bed and calls for Mommy and Daddy. They greet him warmly and celebrate how hard he tried. Limi proudly announces, “I did it! I slept in my BIG BOY BED!” Jazz cheers for him, and the family celebrates together.

## Story direction

Show Maya and Limi Sr. noticing and responding to Limi’s distress each time he needs comfort. Give Limi room to feel nervous and ask for help. Keep the celebration focused on his effort and growing confidence, with room for harder nights along the way.
