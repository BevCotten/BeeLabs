# Adventures with Limi

**Adventures with Limi** is a warm, colorful preschool series for children ages 2–4. The show follows Limi as he encounters new experiences, learns to listen to his body, uses his words, and discovers that courage can happen one small step at a time.

## Current release

### Episode 001 — Limi's First Haircut

Limi feels nervous about his first haircut. With support from Maya, Limi Sr., Jazz, Ms. Toni, and Tiggy, he practices what to expect, asks questions, takes a break when his body needs one, and finishes feeling proud.

- **Published:** September 27, 2026
- **Runtime:** 9:35
- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/FiwvtEJSlbk
- **Core lesson:** A child can feel nervous and brave at the same time.
- **Coping tools:** Ask questions, breathe slowly, request a break, move the body, and take a new experience one step at a time.

## Brand

- **Visual style:** Warm, expressive 3D animation at Limi's eye level
- **Signature colors:** Purple and electric lime
- **Audience:** Preschool children and their families
- **Main cast:** Limi, Maya, Limi Sr., Jazz, Ms. Toni, and Tiggy

## Project structure

- `assets/` — Character concepts and core visual references
- `audio/` — Theme music, dialogue recordings, ambience, and sound effects
- `episodes/` — Episode scripts, recording sheets, cue sheets, and release notes
- `storyboards/` — Storyboard documents and approved panels
- `brand/youtube/` — Channel artwork, thumbnail, profile picture, and watermark
- `social/shorts/` — Web-ready promotional Shorts previews

## Production standards moving forward

1. Finalize and label dialogue before animation.
2. Keep only one visible speaking character in complex dialogue shots.
3. Use separate close-ups for speaker changes and reaction shots.
4. Lock character references, wardrobe, props, backgrounds, and lighting before generation.
5. Keep Tiggy clearly inanimate with fixed plush eyes and no facial movement.
6. Preserve safe framing so vertical promotional edits do not crop faces.
7. Favor fewer, stronger shots over excessive micro-cuts.
